## Flags for your QC

1. **Q7** -- I initially misread the extracted text as a sine wave; the figure shows pulsating DC, so the device is a DC generator (commutator), not an AC generator. Physics and answer key now agree. Worth a look at the reworded device options.
2. **Q11 "transmutation, not fusion"** -- Li splitting into He + H is transmutation. NESA's option wording (fusion vs transmutation) is preserved.
3. **Q17** -- kept as MC because the reasoning (Lorentz transformation of two spatially separated events) does not reduce cleanly to a short statement. Check the option wording reads unambiguously.
4. **Q20** -- the answer is "net force magnitude constant" (A), which is counter-intuitive; it follows from the wall being frictionless (no work done, so horizontal speed and hence the normal force are constant). Reasoning spelled out in the hint.
5. **true-false share** -- 11 of 38 (29%), similar to the 2019 batch. Four near-tautological true-false multiplications were dropped before this preview.
6. **Multiplication rate** -- 20 originals -> 38 questions (1.9x), same as 2019. Every added question reuses the original stimulus image or context.
