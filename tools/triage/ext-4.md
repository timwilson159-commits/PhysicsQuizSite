## Flags for your QC

1. **No quarantine.** Every question was recoverable. Eleven items were originally
   "choose the correct graph / diagram / vector / equation" and have been rewritten
   as worded questions on the same physics with the same answer. I verified my
   reconstructions of Q4 (the F-vs-v curve) and Q8/Q9 (the generator emf graph)
   against the extracted image files.
2. **Grid vs content mismatches.** The mapping grid places Q5, Q14 and Q19 in
   Module 7 (PH12-14), but their content is clearly Module 8: Q5 is Thomson's
   charge-to-mass experiment (8.2), Q14 is electron-positron annihilation (8.5),
   Q19 is a de Broglie wavelength (8.3). I followed the content, per your
   instruction to use judgement. Move them back to Module 7 inquiries if you
   prefer to match the grid.
3. **Very true-false heavy (15 of 29).** This is a direct consequence of the paper
   being mostly figure-selection questions; once the figures are gone, true-false
   is the cleanest faithful form. Q10 was moved to word-bank for variety. Tell me
   if you want more converted.
4. **Q11 nuclear-equation options** were mangled in the PDF (subscripts and
   superscripts scrambled). Rewritten as plain-language descriptions of each decay
   mode; the correct answer (beta-minus, Z 27 -> 28) is unchanged.
5. **Q13 multiplier.** The true-false says increasing the mirror separation also
   increases the deflection angle. That is genuinely true physics, even though the
   original exam treated "spin the mirror faster" (option C) as the single best
   answer and "increase the separation" (option A) as a distractor.
