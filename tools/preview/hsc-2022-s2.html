On a Hertzsprung-Russell diagram, star X has a higher surface temperature than star Y, but a lower luminosity. What kind of stars are X and Y?
  • ✓ X is a hot but faint star (such as a white dwarf); Y is a cool but very bright star (such as a red giant)

  • ✓ In Y (a red giant), helium is fusing into carbon (and heavier elements); in X (a white dwarf) there is essentially no fusion at all

#3multiple-choicefrom Q22inquiry 6.3
Which design feature of a transformer improves its efficiency, and why?
  • ✓ The iron core is built from thin insulated laminations, which greatly reduces the eddy currents (and the heat they generate) circulating in the core
  • The core is made of many separate pieces so magnetic flux can escape between them
  • The copper wire is made as thin as possible to increase its resistance
  • The primary and secondary coils are wound on separate cores so they do not interfere
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): Laminating the core, using thick low-resistance wire, and keeping a continuous closed core for full flux linkage all cut losses.

  • The apple travels due east only, since that is the direction it was thrown

#15multiple-choicefrom Q32ainquiry 6.3
An exercise bike uses magnets on either side of a spinning thick aluminium flywheel to make pedalling harder. What is the principle involved?
  • ✓ The moving aluminium experiences a changing magnetic flux, which induces eddy currents in it; by Lenz's law these currents oppose the motion, producing a retarding torque
  • The magnets physically rub against the flywheel, creating friction
  • The aluminium becomes permanently magnetised and sticks to the magnets
  • The magnets change the flywheel's mass, making it harder to turn
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): This is magnetic (eddy-current) braking: no contact, but a real opposing force whenever the wheel moves.
