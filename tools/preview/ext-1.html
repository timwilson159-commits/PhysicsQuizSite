An object moves around a circular path at constant speed. At any instant, in which direction is the object's acceleration?
  • ✓ Towards the centre of the circle
  • Along the direction of motion (tangent to the circle)

A charged particle moves through a uniform magnetic field. In which case does it experience zero magnetic force?
  • ✓ When it moves parallel to the magnetic field
  • When it moves perpendicular to the magnetic field
  • When it moves at 45 degrees to the magnetic field
