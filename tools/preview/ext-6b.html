The diagram shows a straight conductor carrying a current from left to right (from the + terminal to the - terminal). The dots represent a magnetic field directed out of the page. In which direction is the force on the conductor?
  • ✓ Down the page
  • Up the page
  • Into the page

#2multiple-choicefrom Q6inquiry 6.3
ext6-q6-magnet-plunge.png
A student plunges a bar magnet into a coil. Which of the four situations shown induces the greatest emf in the coil?
  • ✓ Situation D: the most turns on the coil and the fastest, strongest plunge
  • Situation A: only a few turns, magnet moving slowly
  • Situation B: the magnet held stationary just above the coil
  • Situation C: a north pole moving slowly into a coil with few turns
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): $\varepsilon = -N\dfrac{\Delta \Phi}{\Delta t}$: more turns and a faster, larger change of flux both increase the emf.
#3true-falsefrom Q7inquiry 5.3
ext6-q7-elliptical-orbit.png
The diagram shows four identical satellites O, P, Q and R on the same elliptical orbit around Earth. Satellite P, the one closest to Earth, has the greatest kinetic energy of the four.
  • ✓ True
  • False
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): Total mechanical energy is conserved, so kinetic energy is greatest where gravitational potential energy is lowest, which is closest to Earth.
#4multiple-choicefrom Q8inquiry 8.1
ext6-q8-hr-diagram.png
Four stars A, B, C and D are marked on the Hertzsprung-Russell diagram shown. Which star generates most of its energy through the proton-proton chain?
  • ✓ Star C, a mid-main-sequence star similar to the Sun
  • Star A, a hot, very luminous, high-mass main-sequence star
  • Star B, a cool giant off the main sequence
  • Star D
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): High-mass main-sequence stars (like A) run mainly on the CNO cycle; giants and stars off the main sequence do not run mainly on the proton-proton chain.

  • ✓ True

#6multiple-choicefrom Q11inquiry 5.1

A tennis ball is hit vertically upward off a racket (start), reaches its maximum height, and falls back onto the racket (finish), as shown. Ignoring air resistance, which statement correctly describes the ball's acceleration during the whole flight?
  • ✓ It is a constant 9.8 m s$^{-2}$ directed downward throughout, including at the maximum-height position
  • It is zero at the maximum-height position
  • It decreases as the ball rises and increases as it falls

The diagram shows Maxwell's model of an electromagnetic wave, with perpendicular oscillating electric and magnetic fields propagating together. What could Maxwell use this model to determine?
  • ✓ The speed of light
  • The speed of sound in air
  • The frequency of a bat's sonar
  • The speed of ocean waves

#8multiple-choicefrom Q17inquiry 7.3

The table gives the peak emission wavelength of four blackbodies. Which blackbody has the highest surface temperature?
  • ✓ Blackbody A, peak wavelength 380 nm
  • Blackbody B, peak wavelength 724 nm
  • Blackbody C, peak wavelength 580 nm
  • Blackbody D, peak wavelength 483 nm
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): Wien's law, $T = \dfrac{b}{\lambda_{max}}$: the shortest peak wavelength corresponds to the highest temperature.
