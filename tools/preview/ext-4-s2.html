#1multiple-choicefrom Q21inquiry 5.3
Low Earth orbit (LEO) satellites suffer significant orbital decay, have short lifespans and are hard to service. Despite this, they are still used extensively. What is the main advantage that keeps them in use?
  • ✓ Their short orbital period means they pass over a given point on Earth many times a day, which is ideal for imaging and monitoring the surface
  • They never need any station-keeping fuel
  • They stay fixed above one point on the equator
  • They are beyond the reach of Earth's gravity, so they need no launch energy
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): A low orbit is a fast orbit, so the satellite comes back over the same ground quickly and repeatedly.
#2true-falsefrom Q22binquiry 5.2
A car on a frictionless banked track is travelling at exactly the design speed. If it now goes faster than the design speed, it will slide up and off the track, because the horizontal component of the normal force is no longer large enough to provide the centripetal force needed.
  • ✓ True
  • False
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): At the design speed the horizontal part of the normal force exactly supplies the centripetal force; a higher speed needs more, and with no friction there is nothing to make up the difference.
#3true-falsefrom Q23inquiry 5.2
The work done on an object moving in uniform circular motion on a horizontal plane is zero, because the centripetal force is always perpendicular to the object's velocity and its kinetic energy stays constant.
  • ✓ True
  • False
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): Work is force times displacement in the direction of the force; here the force has no component along the motion.
#4true-falsefrom Q24inquiry 5.3
The escape velocity from a planet does not depend on the mass of the escaping object, because that mass cancels when kinetic energy is set equal to the magnitude of gravitational potential energy.
  • ✓ True
  • False
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): $\tfrac{1}{2}mv^2 = \dfrac{GMm}{r}$; the $m$ of the escaping object appears on both sides and cancels.

#8true-falsefrom Q27inquiry 6.3
Lenz's law states that an induced current flows in the direction that opposes the change in flux producing it. If it instead reinforced that change, the growing flux would drive an ever-larger current, creating energy from nothing and violating conservation of energy.
