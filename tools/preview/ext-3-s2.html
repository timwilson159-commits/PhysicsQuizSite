In a totem-tennis set-up, a ball on a 1.80 m cord swings in a horizontal circle around a pole, with the cord at 45 degrees to the pole. Calculate the radius of the ball's circular path.

#2numeric-entryfrom Q21cinquiry 5.2
The totem-tennis ball has a mass of 60.0 g and swings with the 1.80 m cord at 45 degrees to the pole. Calculate the magnitude of the net (centripetal) force on the ball. Use $g = 9.8$ m s$^{-2}$.
0.59N
accepted within ±5% (relative)
💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): Vertically, the cord tension balances the weight: $T\cos45^\circ = mg$. The net (horizontal) force is $T\sin45^\circ = mg\tan45^\circ$.

#13numeric-entryfrom Q30a-iiinquiry 7.4
For the same meson (proper lifetime $2.5 \times 10^{-8}$ s, speed $2.95 \times 10^{8}$ m s$^{-1}$), calculate the distance it is actually measured to travel in the laboratory, taking time dilation into account. Use $c = 3.00 \times 10^{8}$ m s$^{-1}$.
40.4m

💡 Tip (shown only if the student answers incorrectly): The lab-frame lifetime is $t = \dfrac{t_0}{\sqrt{1 - v^2/c^2}}$ (about $1.37 \times 10^{-7}$ s); then distance = $vt$.

  • ✓ True
