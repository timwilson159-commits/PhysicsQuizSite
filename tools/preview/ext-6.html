#Q2multiple-choicefrom Q9inquiry 5.2
The Rotor is a rotating drum ride. People stand against the inside wall, the drum spins up, and the floor is then lowered without the people sliding down. Which statement best describes the net force on a person once the floor is removed?
  • ✓ The direction of the net force is horizontal, towards the axis of rotation (perpendicular to the wall)
  • The magnitude of the net force is zero
  • The magnitude of the net force is at a maximum
  • The direction of the net force is vertically upwards
Quarantine reason: The marking guide gives the answer as A ('the magnitude of the net force is zero'), with the reasoning 'people do not move relative to the wall, therefore no net force acts'. That reasoning is wrong: a person moving in a circle at constant speed still has a non-zero net force, the centripetal force, directed horizontally towards the axis (the normal force from the wall). Weight is balanced by friction from the wall, but the horizontal normal force is unbalanced. The physically correct choice is the option describing a net force perpendicular to the wall (the paper's option D). Per the project brief this is flagged rather than overridden: decide whether to publish with the corrected answer, or drop the question.
